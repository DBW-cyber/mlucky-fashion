    How outfits are chosen

    An outfit begins with the day's high. We choose the weight of the outer layer from seven steps. But the cold of morning and evening is a different thing from the warmth of midday, so we look at the low as well. On a day that cools down, dressing one step warmer carries you through without strain.

    The pieces paired with each outfit are gathered from the season's trends. At the start of each season, we quietly compare the seasonal proposals of major apparel brands with the coverage of leading fashion media, and collect only what appears in both. That collection is the season's item pool. From it, we prefer the pieces that suit the day's temperature band. (Items marked "Trending" in small type are this season's trending pieces.) Items are listed by product name only; no maker or brand names are attached.

    Small corrections are made for the weather. On rainy days, a folding umbrella and water-repellent pieces are added according to the chance of rain. On snowy days, warm accessories and shoes that do not slip come before an umbrella. On hot, sunny days, UV care is added; on days with strong wind, a windproof outer layer is suggested. When the feel of the day changes greatly between midday and morning or evening, please add one layer you can take off and put on.

    For students (middle school and high school), school days show practical layers over everyday school clothes, with add-ons for cold and rain; days off show regular outfits. For readers in their 60s and beyond, we put comfort first: light weight, front openings that are easy to manage, and soles that do not slip.
