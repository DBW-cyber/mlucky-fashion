About this site

"MLucky Today's Winning Outfit" is a free site that suggests today's outfit for your gender and age group, based on the weather forecast for eight US cities: Seattle, Los Angeles, Phoenix, Denver, Chicago, Houston, New York, and Miami. Weather data comes from Open-Meteo, a public weather service.

The outfit photos shown on this site are AI-generated images; each detail photo is labeled "AI-generated image." Tapping an item name takes you to a shopping search (Google Shopping) where you can look for that item.

The map image is "Blank Map of United States With Borders of States" via Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0), cropped to the mainland.
